Critical Anti-Leakage Rules
  • Exclusion of Target Probabilities: All 8 *_resistance_probability columns strictly purged prior to training.
  • Patient Identifier Protection: patient_id is discarded and never used as a predictive feature.
  • Stratified Isolation: Preprocessing and models trained strictly on training splits without test leakage.
Known Limitations
  • Synthetic Cohort: Dataset is synthetic. Performance on real clinical AST data will differ significantly.
  • Imbalanced Classes: Low-prevalence agents (e.g. Fosfomycin 10.2%) suffer lower precision despite class weighting.
  • Non-Causal Attribution: SHAP values indicate associative mathematical feature weights, not biological causality.
